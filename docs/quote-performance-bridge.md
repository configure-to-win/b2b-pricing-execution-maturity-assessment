# Quote Performance bridge

## Purpose

The pricing assessment focuses on six pricing execution dimensions. The full Quote Performance Scan covers the broader process in which pricing, quote creation, accuracy, cycle time, approvals, release and visibility interact.

The relationships below explain why a pricing weakness can affect broader quote performance. They are not one-to-one score mappings.

| Pricing dimension | Related Quote Performance themes | Why the connection matters | What the full scan adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing data foundation | Product & Price Data; Currency Control; Quote Accuracy | Missing, stale or ambiguous commercial values delay quote preparation and increase calculation and validation risk. | Assesses data-search time, currency handling and the wider effect on quote accuracy. |
| Customer-specific and contracted pricing | Product & Price Data; Commercial Control; Quote Accuracy | An unavailable or incorrectly applied agreement can create rework, inconsistent customer treatment or an unapproved commitment. | Assesses how customer terms interact with quote inputs, error causes and commercial checks. |
| Calculations and deal economics | Commercial Control; True Deal Economics; Rebate Impact | Inconsistent formulas prevent sales, pricing and finance from evaluating the same economic outcome. | Assesses margin, rebate and commercial decision quality across the complete quote. |
| Price execution | Quote Accuracy; Commercial Control; Quote Release Efficiency | A correct policy has little value when the wrong price is applied or an override reaches output without control. | Assesses downstream accuracy, checks and release of customer-ready quote data. |
| Exception and approval governance | Approval Logic; Quote Cycle Time; Delay Cause; Quote Release Efficiency | Unclear triggers or authority can send ordinary quotes into review and leave genuine exceptions waiting. | Assesses approval triggers, delays and the effect of review on release speed. |
| Traceability and performance visibility | Commercial Visibility; Error Cause; Commercial Control; Rebate Impact | Without connected evidence, teams cannot explain decisions or identify recurring control gaps and leakage. | Assesses management visibility and the wider causes and outcomes of quote friction. |

## Important boundary

The pricing workbook does not calculate or predict a Quote Performance Score. A dimension level cannot be converted directly into a result for the online scan.
