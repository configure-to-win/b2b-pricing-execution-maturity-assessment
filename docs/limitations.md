# Limitations

## Self-assessment, not independent assurance

Results are based only on the responses entered by the user. The workbook does not independently verify evidence, observe workflow behaviour or audit the organisation's controls.

Different participants may interpret the same process differently. Record the assessment scope, evidence and unresolved disagreement rather than forcing consensus into a higher or lower response.

## No overall score

The workbook does not calculate:

- an overall pricing maturity score;
- an overall organisational maturity level;
- a Quote Performance Score;
- an average or percentage across all six dimensions.

Creating one of these aggregations changes the published methodology.

## No benchmark

The thresholds classify responses within the workbook. They are not based on an industry distribution, market percentile or comparison with other participants.

The workbook does not tell a team whether it is ahead of or behind competitors.

## Limited pricing scope

The assessment covers pricing execution in B2B quoting. It does not assess:

- willingness-to-pay research;
- market segmentation;
- packaging or offer design;
- competitor-price optimisation;
- econometric price optimisation;
- legal contract lifecycle management;
- accounting recognition or tax advice.

## Targets are planning inputs

A target state and calculated gap do not prove business impact, urgency, affordability or feasibility. A higher level is not always economically justified.

Use risk, volume, quote complexity, dependencies, implementation effort and expected operational value when selecting actions.

## Priority ranking is intentionally narrow

The workbook ranks dimensions by current average and uses a fixed tie-break. It does not consider:

- regulatory or contractual exposure;
- financial materiality;
- frequency of exceptions;
- dependency between controls;
- cost or time to implement;
- strategic importance.

Review the two weakest dimensions alongside this business context.

## No product or architecture recommendation

The assessment does not recommend a product, vendor, implementation partner or system architecture. A process may be improved through policy, ownership, training, data management, workflow design, system configuration or a combination of measures.

## No automatic data submission

Use of the downloaded workbook does not by itself submit entered data to Configure to WIN. Users remain responsible for local file storage, access, confidentiality and retention.

## Compatibility

Spreadsheet applications may calculate, render or validate files differently. Review formulas, validations, charts and print layout in the environment used for the assessment.

## No warranty

The workbook and documentation are provided as practical planning materials without warranty. Users remain responsible for verifying formulas, inputs, outputs and suitability for their intended purpose.
